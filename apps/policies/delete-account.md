# Delete your KoloVibe account

This page explains how to ask KoloVibe to delete your KoloVibe account and the personal data linked to it. KoloVibe is an app for original short videos and local businesses.

## In the app

- Open KoloVibe and sign in.
- Go to Settings, then Delete account. The same request is also under Settings, then Privacy.
- Read what deletion means for your account and your videos, confirm, and enter your password.
- The app confirms that your request was recorded. It does not sign you out or delete anything at that moment.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to [privacy contact address, to be supplied] from the email address on your KoloVibe account, and say that you want your account deleted. We will ask you to confirm the request before anything is deleted, so that nobody else can delete your account.

## What is deleted and what is kept

Your account, your profile, your videos and the other personal data linked to your account are deleted. Some records may be kept for longer where the law requires it or to deal with security, fraud or legal claims. The Privacy Notice will list what is kept and for how long once the retention schedule is approved. [Retention schedule, to be supplied after approval.]

To delete some of your data without closing your account, use the app: you can delete your own videos from your video library, and change your notification and marketing choices in Settings.
