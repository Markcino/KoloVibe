Privacy

{{ title }}

Access and deletion requests from the app. Approving a deletion deactivates the account and signs out every session at once. Each service then erases its own data and confirms. Engagement, Creators, Moderation, Content and Business take part so far, so an approved deletion stays "Approved, erasure pending" and is never marked completed by hand. Approving an access request starts an export: each service assembles its own part, and the person downloads it in the app until it expires. Every service that holds the person's data assembles a part; Business is not deployed on staging, so an export there waits on it. This page never shows the export's data. Retention periods and the retrieval window are not decided here.
