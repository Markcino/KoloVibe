## Recommended protocol

Conduct a short walkthrough for each R1 role: viewer, creator, assigned moderator and supervising administrator. One participant may cover multiple roles; report the actual coverage and limitations. Use synthetic accounts/content and sanitized summaries. No paid vendor, recordings, identification documents, dates of birth, passwords, tokens, private moderation evidence or participant contact lists in Git. Obtain voluntary participation consent; use adult participants for this restricted-pilot research. Any minors/general-release research needs separate review, not an invented age-verification policy.

| Role | Walkthrough | Measurable result to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viewer | Sign in, find/play approved content, like/report, identify privacy controls | Which task steps completed without prompting; errors and misunderstandings |
| Creator | Understand eligibility, upload rights attestation, private review, points and appeal | Correctly explains content permission responsibility and that points are non-cash; observed task gaps |
| Moderator | Review assigned item and recorded reason; explain denied unassigned access | Correctly identifies assigned scope and independent appeal reviewer requirement |
| Administrator | Supervise within authorized scope; explain MFA and audit access | Correctly distinguishes supervision from unrestricted role/points/payout authority |

Before each walkthrough, define success for the chosen tasks. Record outcomes honestly, including failures; do not mark acceptance solely because this protocol exists. Link findings to PRD sections, SRS IDs, an issue, an owner and the decision needed. Do not include deferred business, ticketing, live-streaming or cash-payout workflows.
