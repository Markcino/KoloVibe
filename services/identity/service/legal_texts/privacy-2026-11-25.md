## What we collect

- **Account:** your email address, display name, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and your role.
- **Security:** sign-in sessions, the time of security events (such as sign-ins, password changes, and confirmations of sensitive actions), and limits on repeated attempts. Security events record what happened and when, not what you typed.
- **Content you create:** videos, their titles, descriptions, categories and tags, comments, likes, follows and reports.
- **Business features:** a business profile, opening hours and contact details you choose to give, messages with customers, and reviews.
- **Choices:** your notification preferences, which are off until you turn them on, and whether you agreed to receive marketing.
- **Acceptance records:** which version of the terms and of this notice you accepted, and when.
